Shows where a photo was taken from. Place the camera around the subject, then export the picture to sit next to the photo.

Drag to orbit the guide. Click the subject to aim the camera at that spot. Drag inside the camera view to move the camera. Scroll or pinch to zoom either one.

    Sides: left and right are the subject's own, as if sitting in it facing forward. The arrow on the ground points to the front.

    Camera: the marker orbits its aim point. Pick an aim from the list or click the subject, then set the side, height and distance. The cone shows which way it points, and the camera view shows roughly what it sees.

    Underneath: Low and Under put the camera below the aim point. Raise the subject to show it on stands or a lift. With auto frame on, the guide swings below the ground to look up at the camera. Turn it off and lower the subject opacity to see through from above instead.

    Shots: a list of saved camera positions. Export them together as a ZIP of PNGs or as one labeled sheet. Each export uses the current subject, guide and size settings.
