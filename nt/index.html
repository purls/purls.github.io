The player clears every floor of the leftmost tower in any order, then moves to the next tower. An enemy can only be beaten with strictly more points, and its points are absorbed. Dropping to 0 or below at any point is a loss. Division and percentages round down.

Quick add accepts space separated tokens. Floor 1 is the bottom of the tower.

12 or e12 enemy  ·  +5 -5 p-5 potion  ·  x2 multiply  ·  /2 divide  ·  +30% increase  ·  -3% decrease

The solver tracks the best score for every set of cleared floors in a tower. Since more points never hurts under these rules, that best score is enough to guarantee the true optimum without trying every order. Up to 20 floors per tower.
