Rules

  • Symbols are case sensitive. M is mega, m is milli.
  • Symbols above k are uppercase, those at or below k are lowercase. da is the only two-letter SI symbol.
  • No compound prefixes. Write nm, not mµm.
  • Prefix and unit form one symbol, so an exponent covers both. km² means (km)², which is 106 m².
  • The kilogram is the only base unit with a built-in prefix. Prefixes attach to the gram, so mg, not µkg.
  • Micro is the micro sign µ (U+00B5), not the Greek mu μ (U+03BC). They look alike and compare unequal.
  • A space separates value and unit. 5 kg, not 5kg.
  • A prefix never stands alone and never attaches to a bare number.
  • US spelling of da is deka, elsewhere deca. Same symbol either way.
